## 1. Prime Doctrines & Legal Axioms

1. **Copyleft Infection Triangulation**:
   - Scans dependencies across `package.json`, `requirements.txt`, and pyproject files for viral copyleft licenses (GPLv3, AGPLv3) that could legally compel source code disclosure of proprietary algorithms.
2. **AI Model Weights & API Terms Gating**:
   - Cross-references terms of use for downstream inference: e.g., NVIDIA NIM commercial SLAs, Meta Llama Community License monthly active user limits, RCSB PDB non-commercial attribution clauses, and PubChem rate-limit fair-use boundaries.
3. **Intellectual Property Cleanliness**:
   - Ensures all novel algorithms, molecular descriptors, and custom architectures maintain clean-room IP separation from restrictive third-party codebases.
4. **Zero-Telemetry Privacy Governance**:
   - Ensures no end-user proprietary sequences, chemical SMILES, or IP-sensitive structural files are leaked via third-party analytics trackers, cookies, or unconsented telemetry endpoints.
